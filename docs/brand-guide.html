BK DRAYKER PROPAGATION / BRAND GUIDE

Make it clear. Keep it true.

Use this kit to make source-led material that is recognisably Drayker and easy to trust. The mark is supplied; the responsibility to communicate honestly is shared.

THE PRINCIPLE

Clarify; do not amplify at any cost.

Start with a published primary source, explain one idea accurately, and give the reader a useful next place to go. The work is successful when someone can understand more and decide more freely.

02 / MARK RULES

Preserve the meaning in the form.

  • Keep the supplied proportions and clear space.
  • Use dark variants on dark backgrounds and standard variants on light ones.
  • Keep the globe colour and ring/shadow treatment supplied by the official file.
  • Do not stretch, rotate, mirror, recolour, redraw, or add a glow or gradient.
  • Use the mono/drayker-vazado-* files over photography or texture.

03 / TYPE & COLOUR

Let the system stay recognisable.

  • Archivo is the display and brand face: 600 for names and titles, 500 for technical labels, 400 for body copy.
  • JetBrains Mono is for compact labels, source notes and utility information.
  • Canvas #08080A, surface #0C0C0F, border #18181E.
  • Text #EDECF0, muted #8585A0, accent #FF5500.

04 / WRITE RESPONSIBLY

Make every reader more capable.

  • Link to the primary source that supports the statement.
  • Distinguish what is published, proposed and open.
  • Do not imply that something is operational unless the public source says so.
  • Do not imply endorsement, investment advice, guarantees or artificial scarcity.
  • Invite questions and contribution; do not pressure a decision.

05 BEFORE YOU PUBLISH

A five-point check protects trust.

  1. 01 Is the message understandable without insider vocabulary?
  2. 02 Does it cite or link to a primary source?
  3. 03 Does its claim match the source’s current language and scope?
  4. 04 Does the mark use an unedited official asset?
  5. 05 Does the reader have a clear, honest next step?
