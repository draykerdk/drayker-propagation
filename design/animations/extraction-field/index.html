03A / FUNCTIONAL QUARTER EFFECTS

Extraction field

The standard orbit stays intact while energy moves through the two shadowed quarters.

PRIORITYSecondary
RING FAMILYStandard
SOURCEEngine catalog · extract effect
01 / MEANING AND BOUNDARY

What this animation is for.

Extraction field introduces action without introducing a collector ring. It is the preferred extraction animation when the process matters but the machinery does not.

USE IT WHEN

The visual earns its place.

  • Flow toward a focal point is the subject
  • Resource or energy extraction must be shown abstractly
  • You need an effect while preserving standard rings
DO NOT USE IT WHEN

A simpler choice is clearer.

  • No extraction process is being discussed
  • A literal collector apparatus is required

Configure. Do not rebuild.
