01B / UNIVERSAL SYMBOL

Sun

A luminous origin for energy, emergence and collective momentum, held inside the standard Drayker orbital language.

PRIORITYUniversal
RING FAMILYStandard
SOURCEOriginal sheet · 11c
01 / MEANING AND RANGE

Energy with a clear source.

The Sun communicates origin, light, growth, power and emergence without tying the story to one product. Its corona is part of the original body; the dark wedge still interrupts the light and keeps it recognizably Drayker.

USE IT WHEN

The story begins with energy.

  • Emergence, activation or momentum is central
  • Power and life should feel generative
  • A campaign needs a warm universal symbol
DO NOT USE IT WHEN

The meaning points elsewhere.

  • The story is about place and people rather than energy
  • Unknown states or hard limits are central
  • A quiet institutional context needs the plain core
02 / EXACT REUSE
