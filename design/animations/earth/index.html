01A / UNIVERSAL SYMBOL

Earth

Real coastlines, a blue atmosphere and the standard Drayker orbit. The broadest symbol in the library for people, place and planetary-scale systems.

PRIORITYUniversal
RING FAMILYStandard
SOURCEOriginal sheet · 11b
01 / MEANING AND RANGE

A shared world, not a narrow feature.

Earth can stand for humanity, ecology, territory, global infrastructure, cooperation or stewardship. Because those meanings span many campaigns, it belongs before technical variants in the catalog.

USE IT WHEN

The story connects systems to place.

  • People or communities are the subject
  • The scale is planetary or international
  • Responsibility, ecology or shared infrastructure matters
DO NOT USE IT WHEN

A narrower symbol says more.

  • The message is purely about energy or emergence
  • The subject is uncertainty, limits or the unknown
  • A product-specific scope mark is required
