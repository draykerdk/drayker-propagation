01C / UNIVERSAL SYMBOL

Black Hole

A photon ring around a dark horizon: a flexible symbol for boundaries, uncertainty, deep research and the not-yet-observable.

PRIORITYUniversal
RING FAMILYStandard
SOURCEOriginal sheet · 11d
01 / MEANING AND RANGE

A limit that invites inquiry.

The Black Hole can express complexity, gravity, the unknown, irreversible change or a boundary of observation. Without a collector hull or added machinery, the symbol remains conceptual enough for many contexts.

USE IT WHEN

The subject reaches an edge.

  • Research explores unknown territory
  • Gravity, limits or uncertainty shape the story
  • A dramatic symbol must remain conceptually open
DO NOT USE IT WHEN

It would imply danger by accident.

  • The campaign should feel warm and generative
  • The subject is community, place or stewardship
  • A technical extraction machine is actually required
02 / EXACT REUSE

Frame the source. Do not redraw it.
